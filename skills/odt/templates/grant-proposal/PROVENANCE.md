# Provenance: grant-proposal

- **Source**: hand-curated for `open-document-skills`
- **Author**: open-document-skills contributors
- **License**: MIT (see `LICENSE.txt`)
- **Design language**: A4 with 2.5 cm margins (standard EU/DACH grant
  format), Lato navy-blue headings (`#1B3A57`), Source Serif body in
  near-black (`#2B2B2B`). Outline-numbered headings (1. / 1.1. / 1.1.1.)
  per the standard German + EU funding application convention.
  Conservative, reviewer-friendly typography; reads well on screen and
  in print.
- **Intended use**: research grant proposals for any agency — ERC,
  VolkswagenStiftung, Gerda Henkel, Fritz Thyssen, BMBF, EU Horizon, etc.
  English-first; institution-neutral. For German-language proposals to
  a third-party funder, see the localization in `examples/dao/`.
- **No default logo**: institution-neutral; add yours by editing the
  `Standard` master page or via `customize_master.py`.
