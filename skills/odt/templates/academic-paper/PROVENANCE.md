# Provenance: academic-paper

- **Source**: hand-curated for `open-document-skills`
- **Author**: open-document-skills contributors
- **License**: MIT (see `LICENSE.txt`)
- **Design language**: IMRaD article (Title / Abstract / Introduction /
  Methods / Results / Discussion / References). Centred title block with
  Author + Affiliation lines, paragraph-indent body (journal-typical),
  hanging-indent References section. Lato headings (medium-weight) + Source
  Serif body. Conservative grayscale; A4 with 2.5 cm margins.
- **Intended use**: pre-submission article manuscripts for journals that
  accept ODT, or PDF-rendered submissions where a clean uniform layout
  matters more than a journal's exact template.
