# Provenance: letterhead

- **Source**: hand-curated for `open-document-skills`
- **Author**: open-document-skills contributors
- **License**: MIT (see `LICENSE.txt`)
- **Design language**: institutional letterhead, DIN-5008-inspired layout
  (2.5 cm left Lochrand, 2 cm right). Lato single-font for business
  clarity. Header reserves space for institution name + address; footer for
  contact and bank line. Recipient address block uses extra top margin to
  align with the standard window envelope position.
- **Intended use**: formal business letters, official correspondence.
- **Customisation**: edit the header `<style:header>` to replace the
  placeholders `[Your Institution]`, `[Street Address]`, etc., or add a
  `<draw:frame>` to embed a logo. Add `Pictures/logo.png` next to
  `styles.xml` and apply_template will embed it automatically.
